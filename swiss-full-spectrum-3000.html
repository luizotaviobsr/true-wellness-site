Frequently Asked
Questions

What is Full Spectrum CBD?

Full Spectrum CBD keeps the complete natural profile of the hemp plant: cannabinoids, terpenes and flavonoids. Together they produce the entourage effect, where each compound enhances the action of the others, with THC kept under 1%.

How many drops are in one bottle?

A 30 mL bottle holds roughly 600 drops at the standard dropper calibration. Your physician defines how many drops per dose.

How long does it take to work?

Sublingual absorption typically starts within 15 to 45 minutes, with effects lasting 4 to 6 hours. Oral ingestion slows onset and extends duration.

Will this product show THC?

It contains a minimal amount of THC (under 1%), documented in the Certificate of Analysis.

Do I need a prescription?

Yes. This is a product intended for use under medical prescription and supervision.

Is there a Certificate of Analysis?

Yes. Every batch has a COA documenting composition, purity and potency. Ask our team for the COA of the lot you received.
